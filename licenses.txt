3. PRIVACY POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 Scope
This Policy describes how the CultForge Launcher and related CultForge
online services process information when you use the Software.

3.2 Data we process

(a) Account & profile
    Identifiers and profile data associated with your CultForge account
    (e.g. username, display name, avatar URLs, guild membership) when you
    sign in. Authentication tokens for CultForge are stored locally using
    the operating system credential store / keyring where available, not
    in plain-text UI preferences.

(b) Local preference & cache
    UI settings, cached profile snippets, discover/news cache, and similar
    non-secret state may be stored on disk on your device.

(c) Local system metrics (on device)
    For Performance / telemetry UI, the local sidecar may read CPU, memory,
    disk, and GPU-related metrics (including querying tools such as
    nvidia-smi when present). This is used to display status in the
    Launcher. It is processed locally unless a future feature explicitly
    asks you to share diagnostics.

(d) Network requests to CultForge
    When online features run, the Launcher and sidecar call CultForge APIs
    (e.g. api.cultforge.com) for auth, profile, guilds, builds, news,
    updates, and related functions. Those requests include normal
    connection metadata (IP address, timestamps, user-agent / client
    version) as processed by CultForge servers.

(e) Updates
    Update checks may contact CultForge update endpoints and/or GitHub
    Releases to obtain version metadata and installers.

(f) Third-party services you choose
    - Modrinth and similar catalogs when you browse/install content.
    - Optional AI providers when you configure your own API keys
      (keys stay in local keyring; prompts go to that provider per your
      configuration).
    - Microsoft / Minecraft services if you use flows that involve them.
    Those parties process data under their own privacy policies.

(g) Logs
    Local log files (e.g. sidecar logs) may contain diagnostic messages on
    your machine. Avoid sharing log files publicly if they might contain
    paths or tokens; redact before support uploads.

3.3 What we do not sell
CultForge does not sell your personal information.

3.4 Purpose of processing
Operate the Launcher and services; authenticate users; deliver builds and
guild features; provide updates and security; show local performance UI;
improve reliability; comply with law; and prevent abuse.

3.5 Retention
Account data is retained while your account is active and as needed for
legal/security purposes. Local caches and logs remain on your device until
you delete them or uninstall. Server retention follows CultForge backend
practices.

3.6 Security
We use industry-reasonable measures (HTTPS to APIs, OS keyring for
secrets, integrity checks for updates/artifacts where implemented). No
method is 100% secure.

3.7 Children
The Launcher is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age
required in your region). Do not create an account if you are under the
applicable age without verifiable parental consent where required.

3.8 Your choices
  - Sign out / clear local session (keyring-backed tokens).
  - Uninstall the Launcher and delete its data directories.
  - Avoid optional AI providers or leave keys unset.
  - Contact CultForge support to request account-related access or
    deletion where applicable under local law (GDPR/CCPA/etc.).

3.9 International transfers
If you access CultForge from outside the region where servers are hosted,
your information may be processed in other countries with different data
protection laws.

3.10 Changes
